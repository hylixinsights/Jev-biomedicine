# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-28

- Expanded the maintained catalogue and Supplementary Table S1 from 52 to 65 questions across 13 domains.
- Added EC06–EC11 for surveillance cases, outbreak signals, mapped clusters, SEIR/SEIRS assumptions, reporting delays and forecast-time exposure evidence.
- Added PM01–PM04 for therapy-related evidence, feature availability, clinical complementarity and alternative explanations for laboratory results.
- Added HI05–HI06 for H&E inflammation and tissue pathogen assays, and RI01 for CT/X-ray research criteria.
- Added a guide connecting 25 project examples to 13 new and 12 existing questions, including candidate-gene prioritization, flow cytometry and molecular interpretation.
- Published Word and Markdown versions of Supplementary Table S1 with numbered answer options and provisional operational definitions.
- Preserved all 52 original entries and the archival workbook. New entries have separate provenance; no model execution or biomedical validation is claimed.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-28

- Preserved the supplied Excel workbook and exported all 52 question/context/routing records across 11 domains.
- Added a portable preparation skill, proposed operational definitions, source mapping and revision register.
- Added evidence/context/result contracts, study and handoff templates, and synthetic worked examples.
- Documented version-specific Laya packing, future Jev integration and training-data preparation.
- No models were installed, called, trained or scientifically validated.
